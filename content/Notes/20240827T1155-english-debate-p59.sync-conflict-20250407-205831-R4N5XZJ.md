# english debate p59

> "Social media is sabotaging real communication."

\- Communication executive Susan Tardanico

## Notes for debating the debate

I'm against this statement.

- Social media isn't sabotaging real communication. In fact, it makes real communication even easier and more accessible for people.
- I strongly believe that social media is a very important and useful tool in order to communicate with other people.
- It's also important to keep in mind that when using social media, one can feel less pressure and be more open while talking to other people. This can both have benefits and downside, but nonetheless, it makes people talk more with each other, in a way that could be argued is more real than the "real communication".
