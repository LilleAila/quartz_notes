## The Atlantic triangular slave trade and the Scramble for Africa

Transatlantic slave trade
Three way trade between Europe, America and Africa (the predecessor of [[20250110T0937-global-produksjon|global production]])

America had raw materials such as gotton or wood
Sent to Europe for manufacturing, into furniture and shirts
Europe would send the goods back to America
Africa would send gold and iron to europe to get some of the goods.
America got labor from African slaves.
More than 12 million slaves brought from Africa to America.
Lasted 300 years

Most slaves sent to America. For example today there are many african americans as descendants from these slaves.

The scramble of Africa: European countries colonizing Africa
Between approx 1881 and 1914. Mainly Britain, Francs, Germany, Belgium, Italy, Portugal, and Spain
Economic gain, etc.
Berlin Conference (1884-1885) - the Europeans agreed on who would get which parts of Africa.
90% of Africa was under European control by 1914
Only Ethiopia and Liberia remained independent

Artificial Borders irrepresentative of the cultural and demographic borders
Exploitation of people and resources, forced labor
